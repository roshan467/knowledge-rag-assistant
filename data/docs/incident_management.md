# Incident Management Procedure (Acme Corp, synthetic data)

Incidents are classified from P1 (critical) to P5 (minor). A P1 incident needs a first response within 15 minutes, a P2 within 1 hour. A P4 incident needs a first response within 4 hours and resolution within 24 hours. A P5 incident needs a first response within 8 hours and resolution within 48 hours.

Every incident must be logged in the ticketing tool with a clear description and the steps taken. A post-incident review is held within 5 working days for every P1 and P2 incident. Breaches of the SLA must be reported to the service desk manager in the weekly report.
