# Information Security and Access Management Policy (Acme Corp, synthetic data)

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all employees on email, VPN and cloud consoles.

Access follows the principle of least privilege: staff receive only the access their role needs. New access requests must be approved by the line manager and the system owner. Access rights are reviewed every quarter and removed on the last working day of a leaver.

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the security desk within 1 hour. USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Confidential data must never be shared through personal email or personal cloud storage.
