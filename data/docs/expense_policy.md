# Travel and Expense Policy (Acme Corp, synthetic data)

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for every item above 500 rupees.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 6000 rupees per night in metro cities and 4000 rupees per night elsewhere. The meal allowance during travel is 1200 rupees per day.

Claims above 10000 rupees need additional approval from the department head. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days through payroll.

Flights must be booked in economy class through the approved travel desk at least 7 days before travel.
