# Leave Policy (sample company: Acme Corp, synthetic data)

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 5 days lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave is 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 10 working days and must be taken within 6 months of the birth.

Planned leave must be requested on the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance and approved by the line manager. Unplanned absence must be reported to the manager before the start of the shift.
